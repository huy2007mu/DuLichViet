CAY THANH TICH + BANG XEP HANG

KHONG CAN IMPORT LAI DATABASE (bang cay_thanh_tich_qua tu tao). Sau khi ghi de file, bam Ctrl + F5.

1. CAY THANH TICH  (menu "Thanh tich" -> Cay thanh tich, hoac ?action=cay-thanh-tich)
   - Chuyen da dat = so don dat tour chua bi huy.
   - Cap cay: Hat giong (0) -> Mam non (1-2) -> Choi non (3-5) -> Canh la (6-10) -> Cay thanh tuu (tren 10 chuyen).
   - Moi cap mo 1 qua, moi cap nhan 1 lan (voucher ca nhan CAY-<id>-<cap>-XXXX, han 90 ngay, dung 1 lan):
       Cap 1: giam 50.000d | Cap 2: giam 10% toi da 300.000d | Cap 3: giam 200.000d + uu tien tour moi | Cap 4: giam 50% toi da 1.000.000d
   - Co hinh cay SVG dong (cay lon dan, la dung dua, qua lap lanh, may troi).
   - Trang "Dat tour thanh cong" va trang chu co hien tinh trang cay.
2. BANG XEP HANG  (?action=bang-xep-hang)
   - Hai bang: "Dat tour nhieu nhat" (theo so chuyen, hoa thi ai chi nhieu hon xep truoc)
     va "Diem mini-game cao nhat" (tong diem cac luot choi that, hoa thi ai choi it luot hon xep truoc).
   - Loc theo Tuan nay / Thang nay / Moi thoi diem. Co podium top 3, danh sach top 10, hien hang cua ban.
   - Ten nguoi dung duoc rut gon (vd "Nguyen V. A") de bao ve rieng tu. Khong tinh tai khoan admin.
